What to keep in mind

Team officials are everyone on the sheet who is not a player. Sanctions come from the match reports of the Spanish federation, the French women's league and the French federation's match sheets. The LNH PDFs have no event log, so French men's games come only from the federation sheets. Those cover 2019-20 to 2022-23 and 2025-26 for StarLigue, and most of the same seasons for ProLigue; the earlier seasons and 2023-25 are not downloaded yet, so read the French men's rows as partial. About one in fourteen Spanish games has no clock and counts only in the result and final-margin measures. Small groups, teams in particular, move a lot from one sample to the next. These are patterns in the data: a staff member is usually sanctioned after something has gone wrong, so the next-goal gap may reflect that and not the sanction itself.
